Decision: A line is drawn for one agent, a dispatch record, from its start to its end (to now while it is open; a record with no finish ends when the work was over or when the next record of its role on the item began, whichever came first), whatever its role, and is made by one function, `lineOf`, so that an item which passed through several agents has several lines and the time before the first session and between two sessions is on no line; the `timeline` tool prints the lines while the runner's block of the agent list draws the line on the row of its agent, because the owner reads the list as agents and asks where each agent's time went, the time no session held is nobody's wait, a session that died without a finish would otherwise run on over the sessions after it and take their states, and two derivations would give two sets of numbers ([Agent list](../../docs/design.md#agent-list)), instead of one line for a whole piece of work or a tool and a page block that each derive the segments.

Decision: An engineer's line is cut from the item's state starts (kept by [storage/activity](../storage/activity.md)) and has two kinds of stretch: work, a turn of the agent running, and waits between its turns that name what they wait for (CI, the owner's cards, the architect, other items, a 14900K run, an event, a merge, a person to look, wrap-up), the first stretch being work whatever the state reported at its start, and the stretches where the item was paused replace the state they were in, while an engineer's session the runner reported nothing in has no line, because the runner starts a session by starting a turn, a report made as the session begins is that turn's own, a line drawn for a session the runner did not run would show work that nobody measured, and a pause is time the agent spent that the line should show and not hide ([Agent list](../../docs/design.md#agent-list)), instead of drawing the item's states whole or leaving the first stretch to the state left over from the earlier session.

Decision: A review's line, and an architect's turn's, is one stretch of work from its start to its end, cut from no state start and no pause, because the state starts and the pauses are those of the engineer's work, a review or an answer waits for nothing, and the engineer's wait for it is already on the engineer's line, so a line cut from the item's starts would give a review the engineer's `等事件` for its whole length and an architect's turn the engineer's `等架构师`, and a turn queued after the question would have no line ([Agent list](../../docs/design.md#agent-list)), instead of cutting every role's line from the item's state starts.

Decision: The lines are read through a tool of their own, `timeline`, with one answer for an item (the lines of the agents it went through) and one for the board (the agents running, the ones that ended lately, the longest waits), because `context` answers what an item is in a size its callers rely on and `look` answers where the open work stands now in a budget of lines for the tree, neither holds a history by agent, an option of either would give it parameters that contradict each other (the refusal of a tree option on `whole_view` in [mcp/look](look.md)), and one tool for both questions is the fewest tools they can have, which the redesign of the tools aims at ([mcp](../mcp.md)), instead of a part of `context` or an option of `look`.

Decision: The answer for one item is at most 6000 characters, the bound the whole view's answer is held to, and when the lines of the newest twelve agents are longer the earliest segments are folded, as few as bring it under, into one line of totals by kind for each agent, with how many were folded said in the first line and every agent's totals kept whole, because the answer stays in the caller's context ([mcp](../mcp.md)) and a line that waited for days can hold hundreds of segments, of which the latest are what a reader looks for while the earlier ones are still counted in the totals ([Agent list](../../docs/design.md#agent-list)), instead of a fixed number of segments for each agent.

Rejected:
- One line for a whole piece of work, with the stretches that had no session open marked: rejected because the owner asked to see each agent, not each piece of work, and a stretch with no session is no agent's wait, so it would be counted as a wait of nobody.
- A fixed number of segments for each agent: rejected because the size of the answer is then the number of agents times that number (twelve agents of thirty segments of about 70 characters are about 25,000 characters), far over what a call should put in the caller's context.
- A line for each turn of the architect that answers an engineer, made from the runner's beats: rejected because the beats live in memory only; the wait stays one stretch of waiting for the architect on the engineer's line, and the architect's turns get lines of their own, one stretch of work each, when the board holds a dispatch record for each.
