Decision: The line of a piece of work is made by one function, `timelineOf`, from the item's state starts, its engineer dispatch records, its pause spans and its end, and the `timeline` tool prints it while the runner's block of the agent list draws it, because the owner and the architect look for the same thing, the longest stretch of one job's life, and two derivations would give two sets of numbers ([Agent list](../../docs/design.md#agent-list)), instead of a tool and a page block that each derive the segments.

Decision: A state start is the `since` of a runner report, made by one function for the live write and for the history replay, kept per item in the activity index (the newest 500) and derived from the commits whose message begins `report_state `, because the runner's reports are already commits that the history holds (1463 of the 32978 commits of the live board on 2026-10-19), a restart must give what live writes gave, and work finished before this decision then has its line without a second path and the item does not grow ([Data format](../../docs/design.md#5-data-format)), instead of a segment stored on the item at each report or a read of the item file's history for each request; the cap of 500 is provisional and reopens if a job is seen to pass it.

Decision: A stretch is drawn as long as the time it took up to three hours and a tenth of the time beyond, with a gap marked in it, the stretches where the item was paused replace the state they were in, and a wait with no engineer session open is marked, because one job's life runs from minutes to days and the longest wait must still look longest while a week-long wait must not leave the day's work a pixel wide, and a pause or a put-down session is time the job spent that the line should show and not hide ([Agent list](../../docs/design.md#agent-list)), instead of true scale or equal widths; the three hours are provisional until lines of the live board are seen.

Rejected:
- Storing a segment on the item at each report: rejected because the item would grow with its reports as the log entry per report refused for the runner's report would, work finished before the change would have no line without a second path that fills it in from the history, and the past would be stored twice.
- Reading the item file's history for each request: rejected because an item file has hundreds of versions of tens of kilobytes, so one request would read tens of megabytes, and the page could not be told when a line moved.
- True scale: rejected because one wait of a week leaves the day's work a pixel wide.
- Segments of equal width: rejected because the longest wait would not look longest.
- A block of lines apart from the table: rejected because a title and its line would stand in two places.
- A segment of its own for the architect's turn that answers an engineer: rejected because the board holds no record of it (the runner's beats live in memory only), so the wait stays one segment of waiting for the architect.
