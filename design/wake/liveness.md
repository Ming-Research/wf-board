Decision: Only a session that holds an item whose derived status is in progress is watched, a sign of life is a tool call as that session or an alive frame from its wait script, which the script sends only while its session runs, a connection is not a sign, and after 30 silent minutes the board pushes an ask to the session and after 30 more minutes it tells the PM, the time the board was stopped being added to every silence at a restart so that a restart neither delays nor repeats a push, because a probe held a wait script's connection open 1.5 minutes after its session was stopped, and over thirteen working sessions a session was more than 30 minutes past its last write 0.2 percent of the time against 11 percent at 15 minutes ([Liveness](../../docs/design.md#the-wake-streams)), instead of counting the connection as a sign, counting calls only, a script that calls a tool as the session, or counting every silence from the restart; the 30 and 30 minutes are a provisional choice, observed on thirteen working sessions when only writes counted as signs and reads now count too, and are reopened if more than a tenth of the `no-answer` pushes of the first week come while the session was working.

Rejected:
- Counting the connection as a sign of life: rejected because a wait script's connection outlives its session, as the probe showed.
- Counting calls only: rejected because a session that waits on a script would have to call the board by hand every 30 minutes or be reported.
- A script that calls a tool as the session every 25 minutes: rejected because it hands the script the session's credential and costs a tool call for what one frame says.
- Counting every silence from the restart: rejected because restarts less than 30 minutes apart would postpone the ask indefinitely.
- 15 plus 15 minutes: rejected because a session at work was more than 15 minutes past its last write 11 percent of the time, so it would flag engineers at work.
- 60 plus 30 minutes: rejected because a stopped session would stay unknown for an hour and a half.
