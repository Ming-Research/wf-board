Decision: A record is an item that is not work: it has a head, an optional plan without acceptance steps, targets and a log, but no status, difficulty or pause, it never waits or blocks, and an owned record row always counts as resolved and is left out of its owner's plan counts, and a project's backlog is a record whose plan owns the items it holds, because a record is not work, so backlog work is needed only through a needed item other than its record, and moving an item out of the backlog under an unpaused root's plan makes that root need it ([Records](../../docs/design.md#records), [Ownership, the tree, roots and top items](../../docs/design.md#ownership-the-tree-roots-and-top-items)).
