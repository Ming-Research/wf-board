Decision: The architect is the author `Architect` writing from the session named for the architect, and the PM is the author `PM` writing from the session `PM`, and the two session names are reserved (a credential of another role under them and a dispatch for them are refused), because each author name is also a project whose own engineers write as that author, so the author name alone cannot tell a project's engineer from the process role, instead of the author name alone as the identity ([Project responsibility](../../docs/design.md#9-project-responsibility)).

Decision: Writes under the retired coordinator author are refused by the one path every agent write takes, with a message naming the PM for the process and the architect for the rules, while the coordinator project stays configured so that its items, glossary and history remain readable, instead of leaving the author name usable.

Rejected:
- The author name alone as the identity of the PM and the architect: rejected because the author names `Architect` and `PM` are also projects whose engineers write as those authors.
