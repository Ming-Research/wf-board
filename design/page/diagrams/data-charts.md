Decision: A bar or line chart is a `xychart` in the ordinary diagram fence, drawn by the pinned mermaid library's own chart type, and the page gives the library eight series colors for each of its two themes (the data-viz palette, checked on the page's light and dark surfaces), a clear plate and a height of 360 px, because the three charts the owner asked for (a before-and-after bar chart of a measurement, a trend line of CI use over time, options compared on one metric) are bar and line charts, the library already draws them, the fence, the strict level, the toolbar, the theme, the cache of drawings and the checks all carry over, and the library's own first series color is `#ECECFF`, 1.17:1 against a white page, which hides the first series ([Data charts](../../../docs/design.md#7-the-page)), instead of a chart library with a fence of its own or a renderer of our own.

Rejected:
- A chart library (a Vega-Lite class one) with a fence of its own: rejected because it draws more kinds of chart than any example needs, adds a dependency of several MB unpacked, its spec can read data from addresses and run expressions in text that agents write, and the viewer, theme, cache and repaint would be written again.
- A renderer of our own for a small spec in a fence of its own: rejected for now because the three charts are drawn without it, and it brings a format agents must learn and a renderer to maintain; it is the way to draw what `xychart` cannot (scatter, side-by-side or stacked bars, error bars), and it does not conflict with this decision.
- Images of charts: rejected because the board stores text only and an agent cannot draw an image in its session.
