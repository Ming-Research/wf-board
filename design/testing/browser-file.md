Decision: The browser layer is one file of at most 15 tests that share one browser, one generated board and one page and have no spoiled-page variants of their own, held to 15 tests by a unit test and to 90 seconds by the `timeout` of its step, run by a workflow of its own after each merge to the main line, every night and by hand, and never on a pull request's path, because it keeps only what a script cannot see (the width and place of a box, what a browser hides, hit-testing, scrolling, and what the real diagram library draws), its 15 tests took 48 seconds on a hosted runner and their job 63 seconds, which sits over the 60-second line for a pull request's checks where two runs of one configuration differ by 13%, and a layout break that reaches the main line is fixed there while most of the bugs of that kind that the witness comments name were found by the owner or a reviewer and not by the browser job ([The browser file](../../docs/design.md#the-browser-file)), instead of the browser tests in the full CI, a browser job on every pull request, or no browser layer.

Decision: The time of one press of n in a real browser is reported by the browser workflow (the main thread's busy time over 30 presses on a board of the live board's weight, to the log and the job summary) and judged by nothing, because the speed of a hosted runner differs by up to a factor of two between jobs, so a fixed limit would turn red with the runner and not with the page, instead of the former limit of 0.2 seconds at the median held by the browser job ([The browser file](../../docs/design.md#the-browser-file)).

Rejected:
- The browser file on a pull request's path: rejected because its job took 63 seconds on a hosted runner against the 60 seconds the pull request's whole run may take, and the 13% spread between runs of one configuration would shift the total across the line.
- No browser layer: rejected because nothing else sees a width, a place or a hit, and the bugs of that kind are the ones the owner found first.
- The browser tests as a job of the full CI that some runs leave out: rejected because a run started by hand then has no browser result unless it is asked for, the time gate must tell a run with the job from one without, and the gate could only report.
- A fixed time limit for n in the real browser: rejected because the same page measured at the median of 142 to 205 ms in four runs and the runner's speed differs by up to a factor of two.
